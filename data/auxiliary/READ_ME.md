Here are some additional files/data that are helpful with this workflow. 


* GCAM_hector_emissions_maps.csv - from GCAM2Hector helpful when converting from 
GCAM emissions to hector variable names and also units which is really helpful when 
checking for the continuity 

* hector-queries.xml - a custom query file that I wrote with specific hector related 
queries in it, also formatted using the hector naming conventions 


* C.X.csv - are intermediate observation files used in history4hector 
* the csv files with ar6 in them are from AR6 (Table 7.SM.4).
